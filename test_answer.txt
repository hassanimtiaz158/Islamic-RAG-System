=== Answer ===
Patience (Sabr) is one of the greatest virtues in Islam. Allah says: "O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient." [Quran Al-Baqarah 2:153]

"And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient." [Quran Al-Baqarah 2:155]

The Prophet ﷺ said: "No one has been given anything better than patience." [Bukhari, Zakat, No. 1469]
